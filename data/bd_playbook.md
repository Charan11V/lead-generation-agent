BD Playbook
Segmented by vertical. Each section has the ideal customer profile, the trigger events worth watching for, a follow-up cadence, and ready-to-send WhatsApp snippets. All snippets follow Frequency's existing outreach structure: a specific hook → named proof points with brief context → one low-friction CTA.
1. Outreach principles (apply across every vertical)
Keep the first WhatsApp message under ~350 characters — one hook, one proof point, one CTA. No “Hope you're doing well.”
The hook is always specific to them: a funding round, a new role posted, a hiring spree, an executive departure, a product launch — never a generic “we help companies like yours.”
One CTA per message. Never stack “let me know if useful, or I can also send over...” — pick the single easiest next step.
No attachments or links on the first touch. Earn the second message before sending a deck or case study.
Cadence: Day 0 (opener) → Day 4–5 (value-add follow-up, not a repeat ask) → Day 10–12 (soft nudge) → Day 21 (graceful close, leaves the door open).
Warm intros always outperform cold — name the referrer in the first line whenever one exists.
2. Executive Search
ICP: founder-led / growth-stage companies (Seed–Series C) hiring their first or next CXO/VP. Triggers to watch: funding announcements, a new leadership role posted on LinkedIn, a competitor's exec departure, expansion into a new geography or business line.
Basic intro/opening call pitch
We've been active in hiring for [Domain] space, and we actually worked with [compitator] on a couple of mandates recently.  So I wanted to see if there are any synergies that are worth exploring with [Client]?
Even if there's nothing on the table right now, scaling or scouting roles that are niche are hard to close quickly. Would it make sense to have like, a 15-minute call just to introduce us and maybe when you do need help you don't start from scratch, and you already know who we are? Does that sound good?
Opener (funding trigger)
Hi {Name}, congrats on {Company}'s {round} — saw the news. We've recently closed {role} searches for similar {stage} companies (SiMa.ai, Gnani.ai, hosted.ai) in 6–8 weeks. Worth a quick chat on your leadership roadmap?
Opener (role-posted trigger)
Hi {Name}, noticed {Company} is hiring a {role} — we run search for exactly this profile and recently placed a {similar role} at {comparable company}. Happy to share 2–3 profiles this week if that's useful?
Follow-up (Day 4–5, value-add)
Hi {Name}, following up — happy to send a quick 1-pager on the {sector} {role} talent landscape (comp bands, who's actively looking) even if the timing isn't right yet. Want me to send it over?
Soft nudge (Day 10–12)
No pressure {Name} — flagging that {specific candidate/market insight} in case it's relevant. Should I check back once the {role} search is live?
Warm intro
Hi {Name}, {Referrer} mentioned {Company} is scaling the {function} team — we recently placed a {role} at {similar company}. Open to a 15-min call this week?
3. Fractional CXO / Advisory
ICP: companies at a stage where a full-time CXO isn't justified yet but the function needs senior ownership — typically pre-Series A to Series B, or post-fundraise with a specific gap (finance, product, tech, marketing, HR). Triggers: recent fundraise without a corresponding CXO hire, a departed CXO with no immediate replacement, rapid headcount growth without functional leadership.
Opener (gap trigger)
Hi {Name}, noticed {Company} has scaled to {size} without a full-time {function} lead yet — that's usually exactly where a fractional {role} pays for itself fast. We run a bench of fractional CXOs across finance, product, tech and marketing. Worth a quick intro call?
Opener (post-departure trigger)
Hi {Name}, saw {Company}'s {role} moved on recently — a fractional {role} can keep the function moving while you run a proper full-time search in parallel. We have a few people who could start within 2 weeks. Want a quick intro?
Follow-up (Day 4–5)
Hi {Name}, happy to share short profiles of 2–3 fractional {function} leaders on our bench — no commitment, just so you have options on file. Want me to send them over?
Soft nudge (Day 10–12)
Checking back {Name} — even a 2-day/week fractional {role} tends to unblock a lot at your stage. Happy to keep this light — 15 minutes whenever suits?
4. Capital Advisory (with Onlycapital - Triton)
ICP: growth-stage founders raising equity or structured/venture debt, or exploring capital solutions alongside a leadership build-out. Triggers: recent traction milestones (ARR, user growth), a founder publicly signalling an upcoming raise, existing search/fractional clients who mention capital needs in passing.
Opener (traction trigger)
Hi {Name}, congrats on {milestone} — impressive traction. We work with Onlycapital on capital advisory for growth-stage founders, including structured debt and equity raises. Happy to share how we've helped similar companies think about their capital stack, if useful?
Cross-sell (existing search/fractional client)
Hi {Name}, separate from the {role} search — if a raise is on your radar this year, our capital advisory arm (with Onlycapital) might be worth a quick conversation. Want me to loop {Colleague} in?
Follow-up (Day 4–5)
Hi {Name}, no pressure on timing — happy to just share what we're seeing on {debt/equity} terms in {sector} right now if that's useful context for planning.
5. Leadership Learning (emerging vertical)
ICP: existing search/fractional clients with 30–150 person teams who don't yet have a formal leadership development function — good pilot-partner profile since the vertical is still being built out. Position as an early, collaborative ask rather than a polished pitch.
Opener (existing relationship)
Hi {Name}, quick one outside the {role} search — we're building out a leadership development track (inspired by potential-based frameworks like Egon Zehnder's, adapted for Indian growth-stage teams). Would love your read as a possible early pilot partner — open to a 15-min chat?
Opener (cold, sector-specific)
Hi {Name}, noticed {Company}'s team has roughly doubled this year — that's usually when leadership-bench gaps start showing. We're piloting a leadership development program built around potential (not just current performance). Worth an exploratory call?
